Offline story mode

A big open world map with action-packed missions and a city full of distractions. Race a fast taxi, go bowling, shoot hoops, fly a plane — or just see how far a pedestrian flies when your bumper says hello.

  • Open world missions and side activities
  • Physics-based cars, bikes and planes
  • Police chases up to the five-star wanted level

Up to 16 players

Online multiplayer

Play with friends in Free For All and Team Deathmatch. Unlock character items and weapons in online matches, then carry them back into offline story mode. Funny dances and emotes included, free of charge.

  • Free For All and Team Deathmatch
  • NoobTown, JackStreet and Parkour Playground
  • Unlocks shared with offline mode
